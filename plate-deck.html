Where every image goes, and how it is named

A plate is a figure. A scene is a moment that figure was in. The register could describe the first and had no word for the second, so a file called gw-winter was filed as a man named gw missing his terminal plate. He was George Washington at Valley Forge.

The grammar

img/george-washingtonowner · a roster key-cardvariant.jpg
img/scene/george-washingtonowner--the splitvalley-forgemoment · free text-thumbvariant, optional.jpg

The subdirectory keeps a scene out of the plate grammar, which would otherwise read gw-winter-card.jpg as owner gw, variant winter.
The double hyphen splits owner from moment — a single one cannot, because nearly every roster key already contains a hyphen.
A bare owner--tag.jpg with no variant is the lead, and is the common case.
